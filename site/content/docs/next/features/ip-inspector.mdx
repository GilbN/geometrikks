The IP inspector is a side sheet about one address: how much it sent, how the server answered, what it asked for, where it came from, and what CrowdSec and Spamhaus say about it. It covers the selected time range, and the range picker under the address changes it without closing the sheet.

![The IP inspector for one address, with signals, stats and top lists](/screenshots/ip-inspector.webp)

## Opening it

- From a table, open the IP actions menu after the address and choose Inspect IP. Access Logs, Geo Logs, Debug Logs, Analytics and the Security tables all have the menu.
- On the map, use the inspect button next to an IP in a location's details, in a banned IP's details, or in the Top IPs list of the map controls.
- From the inspector itself, the Analytics and Access logs buttons at the bottom open those pages filtered to the address. On a screen wide enough to show the page beside the sheet, the inspector stays open there.

## What it shows

The header names the address's city and country, its AS number and organization with a link to bgp.he.net, and, when CrowdSec is set up, a link to the address on CrowdSec CTI. Under it, a badge shows the active CrowdSec decision with its scenario and time left, and a DROP badge shows the Spamhaus listing with a link to its SBL record. With CrowdSec write access, the ban or unban button sits next to the address.

Below that come:

- **Signals**, the chips described in the next section.
- **Stats**: requests, bytes sent, average response time, the split by status class, and a bar chart of requests over the range. Click a bar to zoom the time range to that hour or day; Back to returns to the range you had. For a banned address the chart marks the bars that came after the ban.
- **Targets**, the hosts the address hit, when the log records a host.
- **Paths** and **User agents**, its busiest requests and clients.
- **Also seen from**, every other location the address resolved to, each with a Fly to button that opens the map on that location.
- **Latest requests**, its 10 newest requests in the range with time, host, method, path, status and size.

The footer has Analytics, Access logs, Debug logs when the address sent malformed requests, and the button for an [abuse report](./abuse-reports.mdx).

## Signals

Each chip flags a pattern worth a second look. Hover a chip for its explanation. None of them bans anything.

| Chip | When it appears | Color |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `N% 4xx` | 50% or more of the address's requests got a 4xx response, counted only from 20 requests up | Amber, red from 70% |
| DROP ASN | The address belongs to an AS on Spamhaus's ASN-DROP list | Red |
| Hosting ASN | The address belongs to a network on the bundled hosting, VPN and datacenter list | Amber |
| `Still seen Nh after ban` | Requests kept arriving after the CrowdSec ban started. The chip adds how many requests came after it | Red |
| `N paths` | The address asked for 50 or more distinct paths in the range | Amber |
| `N malformed` | The parser flagged one or more of its lines as malformed | Gray |
| Burst | At least half of its requests landed in one hour or day bucket, counted only from 100 requests up | Amber |

Still seen after ban means one of two things. Either no bouncer enforces the decision, or the traffic reaches your proxy on a path the bouncer doesn't cover. The count is per bucket, so a ban in the middle of an hour can undercount by up to one bucket.

## The IP actions menu

Every IP in a table has a copy button and an IP actions button after it. The menu offers:

- Copy IP and Inspect IP.
- Fly to on the map, which opens the map on the address's location.
- Filter by IP, host, server, country or ASN, taken from the row, and Exclude for the values the page can exclude. A value already in a filter gets Stop filtering or Stop excluding instead. Which values appear depends on the page's filters.
- With CrowdSec write access, Ban IP with a choice of 1 hour, 4 hours, 24 hours, 7 days or Forever, or Unban IP for a banned address. A ban or unban waits 5 seconds before it reaches CrowdSec, behind a toast with Undo.
- For an address on a DROP list, a ban or unban of the whole DROP network.

The menu's header shows the address with its CrowdSec decision and DROP listing. In the footer of a record sheet, such as an access log row's details, the menu leaves out Copy, Inspect and Fly to, since the sheet has its own buttons for them.

## On a phone

The inspector opens full screen. The IP actions menu opens as a drawer from the bottom, or from the right on a phone held sideways, and Ban opens a second drawer with the durations, the same one the map panels use.
